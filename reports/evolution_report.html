5. Amazon vs Takealot: Do US and SA Customers Care About Different Things?

Analysis reveals notable differences between US (Amazon) and South African (Takealot) customer sentiment:

  • US customers are generally more positive about pricing, as iPhones are cheaper relative to local income and more widely available through carrier deals
  • SA customers place higher value on premium status and resale value, but are more critical of pricing due to import duties and exchange rates
